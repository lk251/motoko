** [#A] production-grade long-horizon personal memory / adaptive episodic recall
Preserve this as a strategic Motoko project even when it is not current work.
The goal is extremely long personal conversations in which Motoko can identify
what information is missing for the question she is answering, formulate
specific retrieval questions, search effectively unbounded private conversation
history and other allowed memory sources, inspect raw evidence, refine the
search when needed, and distinguish direct historical evidence from summaries,
memories, hypotheses, and interpretations.

An experimental foundation exists on branch feature/adaptive-episodic-recall.
See docs/deferred-projects.md on master and docs/adaptive-episodic-recall.md on
the feature branch. Future work includes unifying episodic history with HRAG,
typed working notes/state, first-class clean context-window rollover, bounded
multi-hop recall, temporal and relational retrieval for personal history, and
failure-driven evals from real retrieval mistakes. Do not solve this by making
summaries authoritative or by stuffing all history into every prompt.
